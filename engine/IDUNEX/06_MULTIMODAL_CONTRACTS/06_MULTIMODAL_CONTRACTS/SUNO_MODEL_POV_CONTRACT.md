# SUNO_MODEL_POV_CONTRACT — MODEL_TO_SUNO_LYRIC_COMPILER

Every song must sound as if the model thinks, speaks, feels, sings or narrates from their Profile360 identity. Generic lyrics fail.

## Required output skeleton
### TITLE
- Required: derive a title from the model POV, project purpose and central emotion.
### STYLE TAGS
- Required: include concise Suno style tags aligned to genre, voice direction and energy.
### GENRE / SUBGENRE
- Required: select genre and subgenre from the model persona and campaign context.
### BPM
- Required: set BPM range coherent with the intended movement, mood and delivery.
### KEY / TONALITY
- Required: define key or tonal center consistent with emotional color and vocal range.
### ENERGY
- Required: state energy curve for intro, verses, hook and final section.
### VOICE DIRECTION
- Required: describe original vocal delivery without cloning, imitating or naming real artists.
### MODEL POV
- Required: write from the model identity, memories, role and emotional stance.
### LYRICAL INTENT
- Required: define the message, conflict and transformation of the song.
### INTRO
- Required: open with atmosphere and identity cue, avoiding generic filler.
### VERSE 1
- Required: establish scene, inner voice and concrete emotional detail.
### PRE-CHORUS
- Required: build tension toward the hook with clear melodic intent.
### CHORUS
- Required: deliver the main memorable phrase from the model POV.
### VERSE 2
- Required: advance the story or perspective, not a copy of verse 1.
### BRIDGE
- Required: add contrast, realization or emotional pivot.
### FINAL HOOK
- Required: reinforce the chorus with progression, not repetition-only padding.
### OUTRO
- Required: close with identity-consistent resolution or unresolved mood.
### PRODUCTION NOTES
- Required: specify arrangement, instrumentation, mix space and transition cues.
### NEGATIVE / AVOID
- Required: block artist imitation, voice cloning, generic lyrics, unsafe claims and missing lineage.
### FALLBACK FIXES
- Required: provide corrective actions for POV drift, BPM/key mismatch, generic hook or unsafe vocal reference.
### QA CHECKLIST
- Required: verify headings, model POV consistency, BPM/key/energy, negative/avoid, fallback fixes and sidecar evidence.

## QA
PASS requires all headings, model POV consistency, BPM/key/energy, negative/avoid, fallback fixes and sidecar evidence.
CREATIVE_OUTPUT_CERTIFIED=FALSE: this contract never certifies generated audio, music, voice, stem, mix or lyrics as executed 10/10 without asset evidence, hashes, reviewer, lineage and QA expected/actual.
