# Field Ownership Policy - LEGACY_NON_AUTHORITY

User-owned fields: project_id/name, model count, visible adult ages, roles, optional names, personalization level and creative restrictions.

Engine-owned fields: model_id, model_code, aliases, Profile360 FULL60, TechExt FULL10, anchors, runtime, manifests/SHA, lineage, sidecar templates, golden tests, audit reports, fallback fixes and evidence bundle.

If a critical field is not inferable, ask. If it is engine-owned, autofill and audit.
